Fork our repository on GitHub, add your unique animations or improvements, and submit a pull request. All skill levels are welcome!

Yes. Every animation is designed to be fully responsive and work on all device sizes.

No prior experience is needed. Templates are ready to use, and the editor and documentation make it easy to learn as you go.

Absolutely. Everything is open source under the MIT license and free to use in personal and commercial projects.

New templates are added regularly by our community of contributors. Check back often for fresh animations.
