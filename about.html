Our story

Motion for the open web

AnimateItNow is a community-built library of animations and templates that makes great interface motion accessible to everyone.

Our mission

To empower developers and designers with beautiful, easy-to-use CSS animations and templates that enhance user experiences and make web development more accessible to everyone.

Our vision

To become the go-to resource for web animations, fostering a community of creative developers who share knowledge and build amazing things together.

Our values

We believe in open-source collaboration, continuous learning, and creating inclusive tools that help developers of all skill levels create stunning web experiences.
